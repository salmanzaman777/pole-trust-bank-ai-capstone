## 2. Consolidated Sponsor approval rule

For this project, a recorded Project Sponsor approval is treated as consolidated approval by all pertinent internal stakeholders for the material actually presented to and approved by the Sponsor. Separate internal signatures or concurrence do not reopen that approved decision.

Functional stakeholders remain responsible for analysis, evidence, implementation and escalation within their remit. This rule does not fabricate individual appointments, approve a later decision that was not presented to the Sponsor, waive law or external regulatory obligations, or create an unstated funding release. New or materially changed decisions route to the Sponsor with the relevant functional evidence.

## 6. Operational measurement specifications

These specifications close the measurement-method gaps. Where Pair 5 did not approve a numeric threshold, the guardrail remains explicitly proposed until the Sponsor approves it after reviewing the stated functional evidence.

| Measure | Controlled specification | Threshold / guardrail | Functional evidence owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repetitive-inquiry reduction | `(approved baseline volume − comparable pilot volume) ÷ approved baseline volume × 100`; use the same approved intents, channels, population and measurement-window length | **Approved target: 35% reduction** | Contact Centre Product Owner; Finance validates benefit attribution |
| Inquiry deflection | `eligible assisted sessions resolved without human contact ÷ all eligible assisted sessions × 100`; report as a diagnostic supporting the approved inquiry-reduction outcome | No separate source-approved target; baseline and report trend without substituting it for the 35% outcome | Contact Centre Product Owner and Finance |
| Human-handoff accuracy | Correct destination, context transfer and recovery action in a representative labelled sample | **Proposed ≥95%**, derived from the supplied digital-assistant reference; Sponsor decision under DEC-08 | Contact Centre Operations with Risk and Customer Experience evidence |
| CSAT movement | Compare like-for-like pre-pilot and pilot responses; report response volume, segment mix, complaints and accessibility findings | **Proposed guardrail: no deterioration from the approved baseline**; any deterioration requires remediation and gate decision | Customer Experience and Digital Product Owner |
| Audit-trail completeness | Mandatory fields cover source/input reference, model/version, output, timestamp, user/system identity, human action/override, escalation and retention status where applicable | **Proposed 100% mandatory-field completeness in the acceptance sample; any missing critical field fails the gate** | Security/Privacy with Model Risk and assurance evidence |
