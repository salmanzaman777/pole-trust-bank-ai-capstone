## 1. Purpose and operating rule

This register identifies the people and groups who can affect, are affected by, or must provide evidence for the approved Pair 5 programme. It records their interests, influence, current stance, desired engagement and a specific engagement action. It must be updated by the assigned Project Manager or AI PMO rather than treated as a one-time submission.

Only the Project Sponsor is personally named because that identity is evidenced in the approved source package. Other stakeholders remain identified by role or function until formally appointed. “Not evidenced” is deliberate: it prevents an assumed attitude from being presented as a fact and creates a validation action for the first engagement cycle.

Personal names and contact details may be added only from an authoritative appointment or directory. Operational contact details belong in a controlled private appendix, not the public HTML.

## 6. Monthly review and escalation protocol

Salman Zaman, as working Project/Program Manager and document custodian, will coordinate this review monthly and before every phase gate; the AI PMO assumes operational maintenance when constituted. The review will:

1. confirm additions, removals, appointments and role changes;
2. validate power, interest, current stance and desired engagement;
3. record completed actions, unresolved concerns and evidence;
4. test whether resistance, decision delay or stakeholder fatigue threatens a milestone;
5. update engagement ownership and cadence; and
6. escalate any high-power resistant stakeholder or missed evidence/decision action to the Sponsor; the Steering Committee supports review in its advisory or delegated capacity.

An event-driven review is mandatory after a material scope change, regulatory finding, pilot incident, vendor appointment, executive change, funding decision or movement between programme phases.
