### Section 1 — Selection Method & Weighted Criteria

Each option is rated from 1 (weak) to 5 (very strong). Weighted points equal **rating ÷ 5 × criterion weight**. Scores are comparative judgments based only on the supplied BC/FS evidence; they do not replace executive risk appetite or due diligence.

| Criterion                   | Weight | Evidence Tested                                                                   |
|-----------------------------|--------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Strategic alignment         | 20%    | Digital transformation, customer experience, risk and leadership decision-making. |
| Business impact             | 20%    | Cost, revenue, risk reduction, productivity and customer outcomes.                |
| Technical feasibility       | 15%    | Architecture, data readiness, integration and security realism.                   |
| Operational feasibility     | 15%    | Readiness, change impact, training and likely adoption.                           |
| Economic feasibility        | 15%    | CAPEX, OPEX, TCO, NPV, ROI horizon and sensitivity.                               |
| Risk & compliance           | 10%    | Privacy, regulatory, model, bias and operational controls.                        |
| Scalability & time to value | 5%     | Enterprise reach and credible early benefits.                                     |

**Scoring assumptions:** ratings measure the quality and attractiveness of the documented proposal, not certainty of delivery. A high risk/compliance score means risks are comparatively manageable and mitigations are credible; it does not mean risk is absent. Ties are resolved through trade-off analysis, not by changing weights after scoring.

### Section 4 — Business Case Summary

Pole Trust has fragmented AI experiments without unified governance or benefits tracking, rising service costs, slow manual lending, high fraud-alert workload and inconsistent employee AI practices.

#### Proposed Phased Solution

1.  Internal productivity copilot and AI governance foundation.
2.  Customer digital banking assistant.
3.  Lending decision-support pilot for completeness and risk summaries.
4.  Fraud alert prioritization and investigator assist.

#### Expected Outcomes

-   35% reduction in repetitive customer inquiries.
-   30% reduction in internal report-preparation time.
-   35% reduction in lending documentation rework.
-   25% reduction in fraud false positives in pilot scope.
-   Above 60% adoption in target groups and zero critical compliance breaches during pilots.

#### Market & Operating Context

-   2.1M customers, 4,800 employees, 160 branches and five regional markets.
-   Synthetic executive survey: 88% identify AI capability as a strategic priority.
-   Digital-support demand is growing 18% annually.
-   Operations analysis indicates 25–35% of repetitive workflows can be AI-assisted.
-   Peer benchmark: competing banks are moving from isolated pilots to enterprise AI programmes.

#### Stakeholders by Designation

Project Sponsor; Executive Steering Committee; Chief Digital Officer; Chief Information Officer and Enterprise Architecture; Risk and Compliance; Retail Banking; SME Lending; Fraud Operations; Contact Centre; Human Resources and Training; Employees; Customers; and External AI Vendor.

**Named sponsor:** Sheikh Moeen Akhtar. All other parties are intentionally identified by title or function only, as directed.

#### Source Assumptions

-   Executive sponsorship is available for an enterprise programme.
-   Funding can be released in phases based on performance gates.
-   Bank systems expose APIs or integration services.
-   Compliance supports pilot-based controlled implementation.
-   Waterfall and Agile artifacts will be prepared throughout the capstone.

#### Source Dependencies

-   AI governance approval and enterprise-architecture support.
-   Vendor selection, data classification and security review.
-   Business-owner availability.
-   Change-management and training resources.

**Architecture and governance:** an approved enterprise AI platform, API integration layer, governed data controls, Agile squads for MVP delivery and an executive steering committee for scope, funding, risk, benefit and phase-gate decisions. This reflects the supplied Pair 5 design; vendor products and deployment locations remain undecided.

**PMI Waterfall + Agile operating model:** Waterfall governance controls charter authorization, architecture approval, phased funding, risk acceptance and exit gates. Within each authorized phase, Agile squads deliver and refine the MVP through sprints, product-owner reviews and measured pilot feedback. No sprint output advances to scale until the applicable Waterfall gate accepts its security, compliance, value and adoption evidence.
