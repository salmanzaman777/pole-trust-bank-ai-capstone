## 2. Recommendation — Relevant Stakeholder Coverage

The pre-refinement 22 grouped entries required functional separation rather than padding. The refined register contains only stakeholders with a demonstrable decision, control, delivery, acceptance, operational, benefit or affected-party relationship to the approved Pair 5 programme.

The refined stakeholder universe should cover:

- executive governance and programme control;
- digital, operations, finance and business ownership;
- technology, data, architecture and production support;
- Risk, Model Risk, Compliance, Legal, Privacy and Information Security;
- Internal Audit and Quality/Test Assurance;
- Contact Centre, Branch Operations, Lending and Fraud Operations;
- HR, training, change, employees and union/employee representatives;
- customers, Customer Experience, complaints and accessibility representation;
- Procurement, Third-Party Risk and the future AI vendor; and
- external regulatory obligations managed through Legal and Compliance.

### Recommendation principles

1. **Relevance before volume:** include a stakeholder only when a traceable relationship to Pair 5 exists.
2. **Designation before invention:** retain role titles until an authoritative source supplies the individual’s name.
3. **Separate accountabilities:** do not combine control functions when they hold different review or approval authority.
4. **Evidence before stance:** record “not evidenced — validate” instead of assuming support or resistance.
5. **One accountable authority:** each decision and deliverable must ultimately have one accountable role.
6. **Controlled personal data:** keep private contact information out of the publicly deployed HTML.
